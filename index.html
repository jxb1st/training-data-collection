  1. arXiv 2309.13952chapter file (2 GB JSON) and videos are public on the Hub

    VidChapters-7M

    User-written chapters (timestamp and title) scraped from YouTube videos; the tasks derived from them are chapter generation, chapter generation with given boundaries, and chapter grounding.

    817K videos, 7M chapters, chapter generation and grounding; 9 examples

  2. arXiv 2110.07058license agreement

    Ego4D

    3,670 hours of egocentric video with dense narrations and benchmark annotations: episodic memory (NLQ, MQ, VQ), hands and objects, AV diarization, social, forecasting, and the later Goal-Step steps.

    3,670 h, 3.85M narrations + benchmarks, narrations + 5 benchmark families; 9 examples

  3. arXiv 2410.02713public on the Hub (academic use)

    LLaVA-Video-178K

    Synthetic instruction data for video LLMs: a detailed caption, multi-turn open-ended QA and multiple-choice QA for each clip, generated with GPT-4o over clips from academic datasets and YouTube.

    178K clips, 1.6M rows, caption, open-ended QA, multiple-choice QA; 32 examples
