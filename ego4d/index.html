Ego4D

3,670 hours of egocentric video with dense narrations and benchmark annotations: episodic memory (NLQ, MQ, VQ), hands and objects, AV diarization, social, forecasting, and the later Goal-Step steps.

Statistics

  • 3,670 hours of egocentric video from 931 camera wearers in 74 locations across 9 countries.
  • 3,850,000 narration sentences, written by annotators with timestamps.
  • Benchmark annotations (paper figures): Episodic memory: VQ-2D: 432.9 h, 5,831 clips, 22,602 queries; Episodic memory: VQ-3D: 13 h, 159 clips; Episodic memory: Moments (MQ): 328.7 h, 2,522 clips, ~22.2k instances, 110 categories; Episodic memory: NLQ: 227.1 h, 1,659 clips, 19.2K queries; Hands and objects: 196.2 h, 88,585 clips of 8 s; Forecasting: 110.5 h, 1,498 clips; AV diarization: 47.7 h, 572 clips; Social: 47.7 h, 572 clips.
  • license agreement at ego4d-data.org, AWS credentials by email, downloaded with the ego4d CLI; not on the cluster.

Narrations

Dense timestamped sentences written by annotators; the camera wearer is tagged #C, other people #X, #Y. Examples quoted in the paper:

  • #C opens the washing-machine
  • #C picks up the detergent
  • #C checks mobile while #X drives the car
  • #C passes a card to #Y

Episodic memory: natural language queries (NLQ)

Each query comes with its clip and the answer interval (clip_start_sec, clip_end_sec) and a query template. Examples quoted in the paper:

  • What did I pick up before leaving the party?
  • What did I put in the drawer?
  • When did I read to my children?
  • Where did I put X?

Episodic memory: visual queries (VQ) and moments (MQ)

A visual query is a crop of an object; the answer is its last occurrence in the clip. Moments are 110 activity categories with start and end times (about 22.2k instances over 328.7 hours).

  • Where is this [picture of my keys]?

Hands and objects

88,585 clips of 8 s with the point of no return of a state change, pre- and post-condition frames, and hand boxes (fho_*.json).

Forecasting

Short-term object interaction anticipation (what and when) and long-term action anticipation over 110.5 hours (fho_sta_*.json, fho_lta_*.json).

AV diarization and social

Who is speaking when, transcripts, and who is looking at or talking to the camera wearer; 572 clips, 47.7 hours (av_*.json).
