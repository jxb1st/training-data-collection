VidChapters-7M

User-written chapters (timestamp and title) scraped from YouTube videos; the tasks derived from them are chapter generation, chapter generation with given boundaries, and chapter grounding.

Links
project page, Chapter-Llama release on the Hub
Annotation
per video: duration, title, description, chapters as {start second: title}; ASR transcripts separately
Videos
YouTube ids only in the original release; the Chapter-Llama release redistributes the videos of its subsets
Access
chapter file (2 GB JSON) and videos are public on the Hub

Statistics

  • The paper's full set has 817K videos with 7M user-written chapters.
  • The Chapter-Llama release adds per-subset chapter files and videos; s, m and l are its short, medium and long validation subsets of 100 videos each.
  • l100_val100 videos1,041 chapters, median 8 per video; mean 2568 s, median 2582 s, max 3598 s
  • m100_val100 videos807 chapters, median 7 per video; mean 1245 s, median 1214 s, max 1763 s
  • s100_val100 videos640 chapters, median 6 per video; mean 498 s, median 498 s, max 898 s
